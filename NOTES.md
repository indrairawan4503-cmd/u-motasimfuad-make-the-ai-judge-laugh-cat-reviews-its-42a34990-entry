# NOTES — Variant B: laptop keyboard (v1→v8)

Concept: ONE specific complaint — the human guards a warm laptop keyboard
all day, pecks at it with ten slow fingers, and shoves the cat off its bed
to do "work". The cat's sting: it wrote the human's last email just by
sitting on the keyboard, and the boss never noticed. Deadpan one-star-review
shape throughout ("One star." … "Would not recommend.").

- **v5r (FINAL) — 197 chars.** Dropped "last" and "by" from v5: "I wrote his email sitting on it." Keeps "My human", the scare quotes, the inversion and the "Would not recommend." close, now inside the gate with 3 chars to spare.

## Final (v5r) — 197 chars, 197 bytes, single line

> One star. My human guards a warm keyboard all day, pecks it with ten slow fingers, and shoves me off my bed for 'work'. I wrote his email sitting on it. His boss never noticed. Would not recommend.

Why it wins: specific recognisable complaint (laptop keyboard, named in the judge brief); voice unmistakably feline (human measured by cat standards — ten slow fingers, the keyboard is *my bed*); inversion turn (cat does the human's job better by accident); deadpan review shape with the sting in the last three words; full byte budget used; no puns, no generic grumpiness, no copied anchor text.
